# The field guide

GAB Premium Ent

For whoever goes out with a van. Everything here is on the phone, and
most of it works with no signal.

**There are two jobs on a van, and this guide covers both.**

The **driver** drives it and answers for the vehicle and its load. They
sign for the goods before the van leaves the yard, and they bring back
what did not sell.

The **salesperson** sells from it. They pick the customer, take the
money, and close their own day.

You will see one of two applications depending on which you are. A
driver has no till and no customer list, because neither is their job. A
salesperson cannot sign for a load. If you are shown the wrong one, your
role is set wrong — tell the office rather than working around it.

---

## Getting in

Open the site, type your four digits, done. There is no username and no
password to forget.

**Install it to your home screen the first time.** On Android, the
browser offers "Add to home screen"; on iPhone, Share → Add to Home
Screen. Once installed it opens like any other app and keeps working
where there is no signal.

If the PIN screen says the account is not active, somebody in the office
has not switched it on yet. That is not something you can fix from here.

---

## Your day

The bottom of the screen has four things, and which four depends on your
job. Everything else is under **More**.

**Selling:** Home · Sell · Van stock · Collect

**Driving:** Home · My van · Load · Return goods

### Home

**If you sell:** what you have on board, what you have taken today, and
what is still owed to you. The figures are for your round only.

**If you drive:** your van, today's load, and who is selling from it.
Tap a name to ring them.

### Van stock

What is actually on the van right now, counted down as you sell. If a
line is not here, it is not on the van — ask the warehouse before
promising it to a customer.

You will not see what anything cost the business. That is deliberate and
it is not a mistake.

---

## Making a sale

*Salespeople only. A driver has no Sell screen — the system will refuse a
sale from them even if they find a way to ask for one.*

**Sell** → pick the customer → add what they are buying → take the money.

### The customer

Start typing their name or code. If they are not on the list, **Add a
customer** — you only need a name and a phone number, and you can sell to
them immediately.

### The goods

Tap a product, set the quantity, and it goes in the cart. The cart total
is at the bottom and stays there while you scroll.

### Taking the money

Four buttons:

**Cash.** If they hand you the exact money, just take it. If they hand
you more, type what they gave and the screen shows the change to hand
back. If they hand you less, it will tell you — take the balance, or
record it as a credit sale instead.

**Mobile money.** Tap the network — MTN, Telecel or AirtelTigo — and type
the transaction id if you have it. Both are optional and both are worth
the ten seconds: the id is what a disputed payment gets matched against
three weeks later, and it can only be matched against the right network's
statement, so the id alone is half an answer.

**Split.** For when they pay part in cash and part on momo. Type the cash
part and the screen works out the rest — read that figure out to them,
it is what they send. You cannot record a split that is all cash or no
cash; those are the other two buttons.

**Credit.** Only if the customer has room on their limit. If they do not,
the sale is refused and the screen says by how much. That is not
negotiable from the van — the office has to raise their limit first.

### After the sale

The stock comes off the van straight away and the customer gets a
receipt. If it was on credit, an invoice is raised for them
automatically.

---

## Collecting money

**Collect** → pick the customer → enter what they paid and how.

The money goes against their oldest unpaid invoice first, which is
normally what both of you want. If they pay more than they owe, the
extra sits on their account against their next purchase.

---

## Working with no signal

**You do not need to do anything.** Keep selling.

Everything you record is kept on the phone and sent up the moment the
signal comes back. The bar at the top tells you how many things are
waiting. **What I have recorded** under More shows you the list.

Three things worth knowing:

- **Do not reinstall the app while things are waiting.** They live on the
  phone until they are sent.
- **A sale sent twice is still one sale.** The system recognises it.
- **If something fails to go up**, it shows in that list with the reason,
  and the office is told. Do not just re-enter it — check with them
  first, or the customer gets charged twice.

---

## Bringing the van back

*Either of you can do this. The driver has the vehicle; the salesperson
knows what went out.*

**Return goods.** Count what is coming back, split into good stock and
damaged, and say what was wrong with the damaged ones. Anything you
cannot account for goes in as missing — writing a number you have not
counted is worse than admitting you are short.

The stock does not go back on the warehouse until a manager approves it.

---

## Ending the day

**End my day.** The screen shows what the round should have brought back
in cash, and separately what went through mobile money — those are two
different things and are not added together. Mobile money never reaches
your tin, so it is not counted against what you hand over.

Count the cash and type what you actually have. If it is short, say why.
A short round with an explanation is a conversation; a short round with
no explanation is a problem.

You cannot approve your own end of day. Somebody in the office does that.

## Things people ask

**Why can I not see what things cost us?**
Because it is not information you need to sell, and it is the kind of
thing that causes trouble if it gets out. You see the selling price,
which is what the customer pays.

**I am the driver and there is no Sell button.**
That is right. The salesperson on your van does the selling. If nobody is
crewed to sell, the van cannot be dispatched at all — ring the office.

**Two of us are selling from the same van.**
That is fine and it is supported. Each sale is recorded against whoever
made it, so your figures are yours.

**A customer wants credit and it is refused.**
They are at their limit. Ring the office — they can raise it, and the
sale will go through after they do.

**I sold something and the stock did not come down.**
It did, on the phone. If you are offline, the warehouse's copy catches up
when you reconnect.

**I made a mistake on a sale.**
Tell the office. A completed sale is not something you can edit from the
van, on purpose — money that can be quietly changed after the fact is
money nobody can account for.

**The app is asking for my PIN again.**
Your session expired, which happens after a period of inactivity. Type it
again. If it does this constantly, tell the office.
